## Building Mastheads

Mastheads is an AI newsroom for publishers, agencies and niche-site operators. Every article keeps the sources it was written from, the checks it passed, its named byline and the editor of record.

I am building the product, newsroom infrastructure and integrations behind it.

## Working in private

Most of my work lives in private repositories. This profile is the public front door, not the full inventory.

I care about software that shows its work: sources stay attached, checks stay visible and automation remains reviewable.
